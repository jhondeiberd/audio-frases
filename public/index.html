Upload your phrase list as a PDF and, if you have it, its audio. With audio, the page cuts out the phrases you choose, repeats each language as many times as you set and gives you a new MP3. Without audio, a natural voice reads the phrases and the page builds the MP3. Everything is processed in your browser; only the phrase text is sent to the voice service you pick.
